🧠 Our Philosophy

At Shrimple, we believe programming should be accessible, enjoyable, and efficient: just like the humble shrimp that inspired our name.

  • 🌊 Our Story

    Shrimple was born from a late-night coding session and a peculiar dream about crustaceans writing code. What started as a joke quickly evolved into a serious project when we realized the potential of a truly minimalist language.

    Founded in 2024 by a team of developers who were tired of bloated frameworks and complex syntax, Shrimple aims to bring joy back to programming.

  • 🔍 Our Mission

    We're on a mission to create the most intuitive programming experience possible. By focusing on readability and simplicity, we're making coding accessible to everyone from beginners to seasoned professionals.

    Our goal is to reduce the cognitive load of programming, allowing developers to focus on solving problems rather than wrestling with syntax.

  • 👥 Our Team

    Our diverse team brings together expertise from various backgrounds in computer science, linguistics, and design. United by our passion for elegant code, we work remotely across different time zones.

    We're always looking for contributors who share our vision. Join us in making programming a more delightful experience!
